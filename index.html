Engineered cooling

Four steps. No wrestling with a frozen wheel cover.

Engineered phase-change cooling. Not a gimmick. Not a wet towel.

01

Clip the shell

The integrated multi-layer sleeve stays on your wheel permanently. Tension-band closure. One-handed release.

02

Slide in the core

The slim PCM insert slides into a sealed internal channel. Phase-change material engineered to a specific temperature point.

03

Recharge flat

Pull the insert out and lay it flat in any standard fridge. No deep freezer. No wrestling a bulky sleeve out of ice.

04

Drive with dry hands

Hydrophobic inner layer. Evaporative outer. Zero external condensation on your hands or the wheel.
